# Conference guest desk: staff guide

Open **Conference guest desk** in the left menu. On a phone, it is the first menu item. Choose your event and select **Open event**.

**Find guest & print tag** now shows a compact guest table. Search by name or email, then use **Print tag** to open the guest's tag actions in a right-side drawer. **Details** shows their registration, invitation and QR status. Tick guests and choose **Send invitations**; use **More actions** for resend and QR management. Searching or changing pages clears the previous selection.

**Upload invited guests** has three steps: download/prepare the sample CSV, choose and check the file, then review the rows and select **Add these guests**. Rows with errors prevent import until corrected. On phones, **File requirements** opens the preparation guide in a drawer. **Add one invited guest** opens a drawer for individual entry. Drawers fill the phone screen, support Close/Escape and return focus to their action button.

Choose the task you need. Use **All tasks** to return to the task menu.

On a phone, the task selector replaces the long row of task buttons. Choose a task and select **Open**. Event settings now has three actions: **Registration & entry settings**, **Invite a staff member**, and **Assign or change staff access**. Their forms open in a side drawer. Use **Close** to return; entered information remains available while the page stays open.

Attendance reporting shows event totals and the guest list first. Use **Filters** to narrow the list, **Details** beside a guest for their full record, and **Category breakdown** or **Replies & tag history** for additional figures. **Download guest list** exports the current filters. Guest-list **Print tag** opens that guest's tag actions in a side drawer; **More actions** opens resend and QR-management controls. Side drawers support Escape and return keyboard focus to their action button.

## A new guest arrives

1. Choose **Register a new guest**.
2. Fill in their name and any available details. Email is optional.
   If you enter an email address, the guest also receives their tag, entry QR and conference information through the email worker.
3. Select **Save guest & create tag**.
4. Select **Print guest tag**. This opens a new tab.
5. Select **Print tag** and choose the event printer.
6. Collect the tag and check the name and QR. Select **Tag printed successfully**.
7. Give the tag to the guest. They present it at the gate.

## An invited guest arrives

Choose **Find a guest & print a tag**. Search their name or email. Select **Print tag** beside their name, then **Create guest tag** and **Open tag to print**. If a tag already exists, open it directly. To print a replacement, expand **Need another copy?** and explain why.

An invited guest can also show the QR from their email on their phone. They do not need to register again. The printed tag and phone QR identify the same guest.

## Add your invited guest list

Choose **Upload an invited guest list**. Download the sample, fill in one guest per row and save it as CSV from Excel. Choose the completed file and select **Check guest list**. Correct any highlighted problems, then select **Add these guests**.

Uploading the list does not send emails. To send them, open **Find a guest & print a tag**, tick the guests and select **Send invitations**. The invitation includes the guest’s QR and a link to confirm attendance. If email sending is not ready, contact your administrator.

## At the gate

Choose **Check guests in at the gate**. Select **Scan QR with camera**, allow camera access, and point the camera at the guest’s tag or phone QR. Check the displayed name and details, then select **Confirm guest entry**. Wait for **Guest checked in** before allowing entry. Select **Next guest** to continue.

If the screen says **Do not allow entry**, send the guest to the registration desk. If it says **Already checked in**, do not check them in again. For a handheld scanner or a guest without their QR, open the relevant option below the camera controls.

## Attendance and settings

**View attendance** shows registered guests, guests checked in, and guests still waiting to enter. Search the guest attendance list or select **Download guest list** to open the list in Excel.

Event managers use **Event settings** to open registration, set a guest limit and choose whether invited guests must confirm attendance before entry. Administrators can add event team members there.

Administrators can choose **Invite a guest-desk staff member** in Event settings to give someone access only to this event's guest desk. Enter their name, email and duty, then select **Create account & send setup link**. Staff receive an email, open its link on their phone and create their own password. The link works once and lasts 72 hours. They then sign in and go straight to the guest desk; they can change their password under **My account**. Administrators can view email status and send a replacement setup link from Event settings. See [staff account duties and setup](CONFERENCE_STAFF_ACCOUNTS.md).

Registration and printing do not record attendance. The gate officer must confirm entry.
